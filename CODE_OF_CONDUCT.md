# Community code of conduct

We welcome constructive contributions from people of all backgrounds.

- Discuss reproducible behavior and technical ideas respectfully.
- Be considerate of different experience levels.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten or disclose another person's private information.
- Never post credentials, account exports or private prompts.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation when needed. Raise concerns through a private contact channel listed on the BambiBear Labs GitHub organization profile. These guidelines do not create a commercial support or moderation service agreement.
